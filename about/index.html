Meet the Founder

Andrew Burton

I founded Burton Business Solutions to help growing businesses improve how they operate through practical, intelligent technology solutions. My goal is to help businesses work more efficiently, make better decisions, and get more value from the systems they already use.

With over 15 years of experience working across workflow automation, systems integration, and data strategy, I've designed and implemented operational systems in complex corporate environments where efficiency and visibility matter. My focus has always been the same—building structured, scalable solutions that support smarter decision-making.

I approach every engagement with a disciplined, analytical mindset. Before implementing tools, I evaluate workflows. Before building reports, I clarify objectives. My goal is not just to improve systems, but to strengthen how a business operates for the long term.

Burton Business Solutions is built on the belief that operational clarity drives sustainable growth.
